Release notes

What changed in each version of WhatsKeep. The newest is at the top.

Version 1.0.1 26 November 2026Latest

  • More apps, Check for updates and the website are now in Settings and About. They open in your browser; WhatsKeep still has no internet permission.
  • The bottom bar now sits flat on the bottom edge of the screen on phones with gesture or button navigation.
  • Choosing where to save or restore a backup or export no longer locks the app again when you come back.
  • Backups and exports now save to storage locations, such as some cloud folders, that refused them before.
  • The WhatsApp tab and the app filter read the archive in the background, so the screen stays smooth with a large archive.
  • Switches show a coloured track when they are on.

Version 1.0.0 24 November 2026

  • First release. A private archive of the notifications you choose, with WhatsApp conversations and saved pictures.
  • Search, favourites, filters by app and date, and changed messages kept as updates.
  • Automatic deletion after the time you choose, per-app settings, daily hours and a size limit.
  • Encrypted on the phone, app lock, screenshot blocking and password-protected backups.
  • Midnight and teal, Charcoal and violet, and Ivory and emerald, in light or dark.

Checking your download

The file for version 1.0.1 is called WhatsKeep.apk. It is 459 KB (469,543 bytes). Its SHA-256 checksum is:

dd46a50e158ac832b6ae986220408b9614f763cb5c3ccc1c1992eeeb30296dbd

On Windows, run certutil -hashfile WhatsKeep.apk SHA256 and compare. Every version is signed with the same key, so each one installs over the last and keeps what you have saved.

Older versions are on the GitHub releases page.
