WhatsKeep keeps it private

WAMR and Notisave are popular apps that save notifications so you can read deleted messages. WhatsKeep does the same job with no adverts, no internet and an encrypted archive. This page shows only the rows where WhatsKeep does more.

What WhatsKeep does not do

WhatsKeep does not alert you the moment a message is deleted, does not save status updates, videos, voice notes or full-size originals, and is not on Google Play, so it does not update itself.

Where the others do more

WAMR tells you as soon as a message is deleted and tries to recover videos, voice notes and other media. Notisave can keep your notification bar clear. Both install and update from Google Play.
