**WhatsKeep 1.0.1** – a private, offline archive of the notifications you choose to keep, including WhatsApp messages.

- **New:** More apps, Check for updates and the WhatsKeep website, in Settings and About. They open in your browser; WhatsKeep still has no internet permission.
- The bottom bar now sits flat on the bottom edge of the screen on phones with gesture or button navigation.
- Choosing where to save or restore a backup or export no longer locks the app again when you come back.
- Backups and exports now save to storage locations, such as some cloud folders, that refused them before.
- The WhatsApp tab and the app filter read the archive in the background, so the screen stays smooth with a large archive.
- Switches show a coloured track when they are on.

Installs over WhatsKeep 1.0.0 and keeps everything you have saved. Android 10 or later.
